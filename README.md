## Greatfit Clothing Store Review Analysis
## Overview
This project explores customer review data to evaluate feedback patterns, recommendation trends, return behavior, and customer satisfaction using Excel and Tableau.
## Business Problem
The objective of this project was to understand customer sentiment and identify factors influencing product recommendations and return-related feedback.
## Tools Used
- Ecel.
- Tableau.
## Skills  Demonstrated
- Customer Sentiment Analysis.
- Dashboard Development.
- Data Visualization.
- Business Insight Generation.
## Dashboard Features
- Review Rating Distribution.
- Recommendation Trends.
- Return-Related Feedback Analysis
- Customer Satisfaction Metrics.
## Key Insights
- The general division received the highest number of customer reviews (15,850), significantly outperforming the General Petite and Intimates division, indicating strong customer engagement within that category.
- Most customers gave positive ratings with "Good' reviews representing the largest share of customer feedback, while bad reviews accounted for a much smaller portion, showing that overall customer satisfaction remained relatively strong despite some negative experiences.
- Over 82% of customers recommended the products, while only a small percentage did not recommend them, reflecting genrally positive customer shopping experience.
- Return-related complaints were relatively low compared to total reviews, suggesting that most customers were satisfied with product quality, fit, or overall purchase experience.
## Recommendations
- Maintain product quality and customer service standards within high-performing divisions while identifying opportunities to improve customer engagement in lower-performing categories such as initimates.
- Review recurring themes within bad reviews and non-recommended feedback to identify issues related to product quality, sizing, or customer expectations.
- Use positive customer reviews and high recommendation rates in promotional campaigns to strengthen customer trust and brand credibility.
- Monitor return-related feedback regularly to quickly identify potential product issues and improve customer satisfaction before complaints increases.
